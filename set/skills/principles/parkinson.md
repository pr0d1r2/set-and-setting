# Parkinson's Law

Work expands to fill the time available for its completion. Set a deliberate,
evidence-based timebox and a clear definition of done so effort serves the
outcome instead of consuming every available hour.

## Applying Parkinson's Law

- Define done before starting. Name the required outcome, acceptance checks,
  and explicit non-goals so optional polish cannot silently become mandatory.
- Timebox work in proportion to its value, risk, and uncertainty. Use a short
  deadline for a reversible task and reserve more time for consequential or
  poorly understood work.
- Break long horizons into small checkpoints with concrete deliverables. At
  each checkpoint, compare progress with the definition of done and cut work
  that no longer earns its cost.
- Deliver the smallest complete result early. Use the remaining time only for
  verified gaps, risk reduction, or improvements whose value exceeds their
  delay.
- Bound research, planning, review, and discussion as well as implementation.
  These activities also expand when they lack a stopping condition.
- Keep teams and processes proportional to the work. Challenge coordination,
  reporting, and approval steps that create work for one another without
  improving the outcome.

## Signals of violation

- A task repeatedly finishes at its deadline regardless of its actual scope.
- The definition of done changes through unrequested polish or adjacent work.
- Research or planning continues after enough evidence exists to act safely.
- Progress is reported as time spent or activity performed rather than a
  completed, verifiable outcome.
- More people, meetings, or status artifacts are added while throughput stays
  flat or declines.
- A distant deadline delays starting, feedback, or delivery of a usable slice.

## When timeboxing conflicts with quality

A timebox is a forcing function for prioritization, not permission to ship
known defects or skip required verification. Fix the quality floor in the
definition of done, then reduce scope or renegotiate the deadline when the work
cannot meet both. Calibrate the timebox with [[consequences]], keep the result
focused with [[yagni]], and preserve the simplest complete solution with
[[kiss]].

Based on [Parkinson's Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkinson%27s_Law),
the observation that work expands to fill the time available and that
administrative organizations can grow independently of the work to be done.
